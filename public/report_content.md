## 📊 Executive Summary

This report consolidates LinkedIn organic performance and Google Search/Analytics (SEO) performance for Nadkarni Speciality Polymers & Coatings Pvt. Ltd. for the period **May 15, 2026 to June 14, 2026**. All figures below are sourced directly from the LinkedIn Page Analytics and Google Search Console / Google Analytics 4 screenshots provided for this period, and from the Google Search Console date range of June 16–22 where noted.

Headline takeaways for the period:

- 📈 LinkedIn impressions grew **405.4%** and reactions grew **741.3%** versus the prior period *(Source: Highlights.png)*
- 👥 LinkedIn page followers reached **1,060 total**, with **88 new followers** in the last 31 days (+450%) *(Source: Followers.png)*
- 🌍 SEO visibility grew to **38.5K total impressions** and **346 total clicks** in Google Search *(Source: Analytics Performance.png)*
- 🇮🇳 India is the top country driving search clicks (236 clicks), while the **United States leads in impressions** (12,739) *(Source: Countries From Traffic is coming.png)*

## 🧭 Section 3: Cross-Channel Observations

- 🔗 **LinkedIn engagement surged** (impressions +405.4%, reactions +741.3%) in the same period that **organic search impressions reached 38.5K** — both channels show strong upward visibility momentum.
- 📉 **On-site engagement metrics (active users, engaged sessions) dipped slightly** even as search visibility climbed — worth monitoring whether new search-driven visitors are finding what they need on-page.
- 🌐 **Educational/technical blog content** ("Ethers vs. Esters," "Simplified Paint Technology 101," cathodic disbondment, epoxy curing agents) is the primary driver of organic search impressions — reinforcing content marketing as a key SEO lever for this period.
- 🏁 **Competitively, Nadkarni is punching above its follower-base weight** on LinkedIn engagement intensity versus Cardolite Corporation, even with a smaller audience.
